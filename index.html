pit-release-gate quick check

This page runs entirely in your browser. It does not require a login or a server. It loads a Python runtime (Pyodide, WebAssembly), installs the public release of pit-release-gate version 0.1.0 from PyPI, runs the package's own included tests, applies the package's release-control screen to a synthetic sample panel it generates on the spot, and prints a verification receipt below. It takes about one to two minutes, most of it spent downloading the Python runtime and the numpy/pandas/scipy packages. This page reports a visit and the receipt text to its author. It sends nothing else.
